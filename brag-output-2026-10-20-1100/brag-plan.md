# Brag Plan: Omnirush

## What is this app?
Omnirush is a one-click research agent: type a topic, watch the pipeline plan, search, extract, rank and synthesize in a live mission-control view, then get a professional report with deterministic inline citations, an integrity badge, and PDF export.

## The angle
Research agents usually ask for trust. Omnirush shows receipts. The video is a fast, tactile tour of the machine doing the work — and the moment it catches a citation it cannot verify and deletes it. Playful print-shop energy, serious proof underneath.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The product's own command deck line, slammed in as giant ink type: **ASK THE SYSTEM ANYTHING.** A cited research run is already loaded beside it. No windup.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The pipeline lighting up in the live run deck: Plan → Search → Extract → Rank → Synthesize → Render, with real telemetry beside it (`27 sources attempted / 26 extracted`, `4 / 8 search calls`).
- The report writing itself: **US$2.01B in 2026**, up from **US$1.51B in 2025**, **33.1% CAGR**, each number carrying a real `[n]` citation pill.
- The integrity check: an unrecognized `[9]` gets stamped **UNKNOWN SOURCE**, then **CITATION REMOVED**. The badge settles on **CITATION INTEGRITY: 26 VALIDATED / 1 HALLUCINATED REMOVED**.

## Outro / punchline
Logo lockup and the actual page title: **RESEARCH THAT SHOWS ITS WORK.** Footer: "Replayable runs · citation-safe output · one-click PDF."

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry:** type `Quantum computing 2026` in the command deck and hit **Start research**.
2. **Key action:** watch the pipeline become observable — stage rail, source counts, search-call budget.
3. **Result:** a citation-backed report with source cards, then the citation validator stripping an unknown source and the integrity badge settling.

## Tone
- Preset: `default`
- Creative direction: a mission-control zine printed on warm paper — sticker labels, hard ink shadows, comic display type, but dead-serious proof.
- Interpretation: quick cuts and snappy entrances, one confident idea per scene, no ironic distance. Humor comes from the absurd confidence of the interface ("mission control" for a research report), not from jokes layered on top.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, upbeat bed with crisp UI accents; sound sells the machine doing real work.
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` at 0.32 volume.
- Music treatment: start at 0, hold under the hook, ride the pipeline ticks, lift into the integrity payoff, then let the final logo hit and fade over the last 0.4s.
- Music cue guidance: bundled vol-10 preset read (109.96 BPM). Beat-grid pipeline nodes at 3.55 / 4.10 / 4.64 / 5.19 / 5.74 / 6.28s; source cards at 8.22 / 8.73 / 9.29s; integrity badge near 14.73s; final logo beat-locked to strong cue 18.01s. Text reveals keep their own readable holds.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; the paper glow behind the report card and the logo halo breathe with RMS. No waveform or equalizer graphics.
- SFX posture: moderate, motion-matched; clicks, drops, card places, keypresses, one impact stamp, one soft bell on the logo.
- Audio-coupled moments: cursor click on Start research; six pipeline node ticks; three source cards landing; citation stamp; integrity badge pop; final logo bell.
- Restraint rule: never let SFX out-volume the music bed; no comedic bonks or glitch noise; the integrity moment is the only hard impact.

## Storyboard

A cursor glides in and clicks at 2.55s.
Sequential/interaction: yes — headline words slam in at 0.35 / 0.55 / 0.75s; a simulated cursor click starts the run.
Audio intent: confident, immediate, a little theatrical.
Audio-coupled idea: cursor click SFX; words land with soft drops; no typing.
Music: upbeat bed enters under the second word.
Transition mood: clean/hard → Scene 2 (yellow diagonal wipe)

### Scene 2 — Watch every stage — 4.0s
Same paper shell, now the mission dashboard. Left: `LIVE RUN DECK` card with the inked pipeline wire and six nodes. Each node pops in sequence: PLAN, SEARCH, EXTRACT, RANK, SYNTHESIZE, RENDER. Top line: `WATCH EVERY STAGE.`
Sequential/interaction: yes — six pipeline nodes light up one at a time on beats; telemetry numbers tick up.
Audio intent: mechanical momentum, building.
Audio-coupled idea: one clean tick per node; soft chip sound when the counters settle.
Music: full bed, beat-grid node sequence.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The report types itself — 4.2s

### Scene 4 — It deletes its own hallucinations — 4.7s

Audio intent: the one hard, decisive beat of the video.
Audio-coupled idea: impact hit on the stamp; soft confirmation bell on the badge.
Music: bed dips under the impact, then returns.
Transition mood: clean sweep → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Research that shows its work — 4.3s

Audio intent: resolved, proud, still moving.
Audio-coupled idea: soft bell on the logo; music fades under the final line.
