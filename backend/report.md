# Research report: quantum computing 2026

## Executive summary

Research on **quantum computing 2026** is summarized from the supplied excerpts [1].

## Finding 1

Quantum computers use quantum bits to represent and process information [1]

## Finding 2

Quantum algorithms use phenomena such as superposition and interference to solve selected problems [2]

## Finding 3

Error correction, hardware quality, and scalable control are important milestones on the path toward fault-tolerant quantum computing [3]

## Finding 4

Quantum hardware research compares superconducting, trapped-ion, photonic, and neutral-atom approaches [4]

## Finding 5

Quantum software combines circuit design, classical orchestration, simulators, and hardware-aware compilation [5]

## Finding 6

Organizations are planning migration to post-quantum cryptography while tracking the future impact of quantum algorithms on public-key encryption and long-lived data [6]
